Audio Clarity ("we", "us") provides an audio enhancement and transcription tool. This page explains what data we collect and how we use it. This is a starting template — please have it reviewed against your actual data practices (and, ideally, by a lawyer) before publishing.

What we collect

  • Account info: name, email, and profile photo from Google Sign-In.
  • Usage data: number of free enhancements used, stored against your account.
  • Audio files: processed entirely in your browser — not uploaded to our servers.
  • Payment data: handled directly by Razorpay/Stripe; we never see or store your card details.
  • Device fingerprint: a hashed technical signature used for fraud/abuse prevention.

How we use it

To operate your account, enforce free-trial limits, activate Premium after payment, and prevent abuse. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Third parties

We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and data storage, and Razorpay/Stripe for payment processing. Each has its own privacy policy governing data they process on our behalf.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your account data by contacting support@yoursoftlab.com.
